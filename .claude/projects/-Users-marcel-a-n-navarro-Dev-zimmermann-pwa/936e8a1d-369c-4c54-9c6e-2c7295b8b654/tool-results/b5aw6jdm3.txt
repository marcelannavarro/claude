## What changes

**1. Multi-colour products stop leaking the wrong colour's photos.**
Before: a card's swipeable gallery could show a mix of colours — e.g., the card representing the Black variant could have Tan photos mixed into its carousel. After: the gallery only ever shows that same product's Black photos, matching whatever SFCC already decided to represent.

Before: if the "official" photo 404s, the card just shows a broken image, permanently. After: it automatically falls back to the next real photo of the same colour — same recovery behaviour already proven on the Rail and Full History.

**3. Hovering a card with 2+ photos now does two things at once, not zero.**
Before: hover did nothing to the image at all (no swap — the data for it never existed — and no gallery interaction beyond whatever was already there). After: the main photo crossfades to a second/model shot **and** the paging arrows appear simultaneously, letting the shopper browse the rest.

**4. This lands differently depending on how many real photos a product has** (checked live, so these aren't guesses):
